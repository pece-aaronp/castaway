How the ratings, confidence and heatmap are worked out

Boating rating — every daylight hour is scored against your vessel on sustained wind, gusts, total sea, swell period (short-period sea scored harder than long groundswell), wind chop, rain and storms; the day is the mean of those hours, capped near the worst single factor.

Confidence — the same day from four global weather models (ECMWF IFS, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON, EC GEM) and three wave models (ECMWF WAM, NOAA GFS-Wave, Météo-France WAM). Agreement = confidence; divergence, models dropping out, and lead time all pull it down.

Bite rating — each species in the zone is scored on season (by latitude band), water temperature, wind and sea for the style of fishing it takes, barometer trend, moon phase, cloud, and whether you can actually get to it in your boat. The day's rating is driven by the best few species. Closures zero a species out.

Heatmap — a grid over the water (land-masked from Natural Earth coastline). Each cell gets a habitat from its distance to shore and whether it sits in an enclosed bay, water temperature from the ocean model, and a boost near published FADs, reefs and wrecks. The colour is the best species score for that cell — hover a cell to see which.

Tide — the curve is sea_level_height_msl from the Copernicus/Météo-France global ocean model via Open-Meteo, at the nearest ocean grid cell. It tracks the official tables within about an hour and is on a mean-sea-level datum, so use it for timing the run-out, not for bar depth. The official link is right there for the real thing.

Solunar — moon transit is approximated from moon age (≈50 min later per day), so majors/minors are ±30 min.

Pixel art — all original, drawn in code for this site. No game assets were harmed.
